| If flowers are… | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| **Too early** (open before 30 May) | Cut at bud stage and cold-store at 2–4 °C. Shade plants with 30% shade cloth. Cut in the evening and chill overnight. Tulips: pull earlier and dry-store. Ranunculus: store in water. |
| **Too late** (not open by 30 May) | Cover beds with a cloche or fleece from mid-April (brings bloom forward 5–7 days). Cut tight buds and open them in warm water indoors: stocks, ranunculus, larkspur and sweet peas open in 1–3 days at 20 °C. |
| **Finishing mid-bloom** | Pick off spent flowers to prolong flowering. Feed with high-potash tomato feed every 10 days. Water deeply. Succession sowings take over. |
| **Frost forecast in spring** | Cover with double fleece. Take in potted ranunculus. Tulips cope to about −5 °C. |
| **Wind or heavy rain forecast** | Cut ready stems early, store cold, and support tall stems with netting. |
