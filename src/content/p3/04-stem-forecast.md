## Sense check

**The totals are large:** about 1,570 stems on the card estimates and about 880 cautious, from roughly 12.5 m² of annuals, 3.2 m² of planters for bulbs and corms, and the sweet pea planters. They are stems cuttable on 30 May, not a season total. The card figures look generous for annuals, so treat the cautious total as the working number.

**Late starters:** clarkia and larkspur only begin about 25 May, and Bridal Silk poppy is borderline. They could give far fewer than the card figures if spring is cool.

**Ranunculus is the biggest single line** (200 to 300) and the least certain: first-year corms in a crowded planter (13 cm spacing).

**Sweet peas** (120 to 192 together from 48 plants on the plot): one plant per cane at 15 cm gives each plant room, so stems stay long. Your 26 garden plants are a bonus and are not counted.

**Tulips and alliums are the most predictable** (one stem per bulb), which is why the early-pull and cold-store plan matters.


**Eucalyptus foliage** is planted out 15 to 20 May but wanted from 25 May. Cut it lightly or buy larger plants.

**Not forecast:** scabiosa, Didiscus, Iceland poppies, clary sage, Achillea and Lunaria (all gambles or later), lemon bergamot foliage and mint (no plant count).
