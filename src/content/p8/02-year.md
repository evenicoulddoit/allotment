Tick these off through the year. The dates match the [calendar tables](#/p3/calendar); where a plant's sow, pot up, harden off, plant out and harvest dates are listed separately, trust those.

## November

- [ ] 1–8 November: pot up ranunculus batch 1 (corms into trays, 3 cm deep), in the frame
- [ ] Finish all bulb and seed sowing by 15 November
- [ ] Plant Geum ×5 in bed 4 by 15 November
- [ ] Line the cold-frame base with 6 mm mesh and close every gap
- [ ] Start the weekly rodent patrol
- [ ] Fleece and frame lid on from the first frost (late November)

## December

- [ ] Pinch sweet peas at 2–3 pairs of leaves
- [ ] Vent the frame on mild days. Fleece in frosts
- [ ] Check stored tulip bulbs for mould and gnawing
- [ ] Check every mesh for gaps. Reset traps
- [ ] Order seed and plugs you still need

## January 2027

- [ ] 3–10 January: plant tulip batch 2 in the other half of planter T. Mesh lid over
- [ ] 15 January onwards: sow onions indoors (15 January–15 February)
- [ ] 25 January–15 February: sow Didiscus on a windowsill
- [ ] Pinch any remaining sweet peas
- [ ] Check the strawberry delivery date

## February

- [ ] 10–25 February: sow eucalyptus. 15 February–15 March: sow lemon bergamot, scabiosa (main sowing), rudbeckia, statice and lavender at 15–20 °C
- [ ] 15 February–5 March: sow peppers (propagator at 20–25 °C)
- [ ] 15–28 February: sow stocks batch 2 (back-up)
- [ ] 20–28 February: pot up ranunculus batch 2 (soak the corms 3–4 hours only)
- [ ] Mid-February: buy planter R
- [ ] Order nematodes if you have not

## March

- [ ] 1–15 March: sow cabbage 'Primo II'. 1 March–15 April: sow chives and thyme. 10–25 March: sow tomatoes
- [ ] 5–14 March: harden off the autumn-sown annuals and ranunculus batch 1
- [ ] 15–25 March: plant out the autumn-sown hardy annuals, stocks batch 1 and Bridal Silk poppy (hardened off) into bed 1 and bed 2. Plant out ranunculus batch 1 into planter R and the Iceland poppies and clary sage (by 31 March)
- [ ] From 20 March: sow carrots and beetroot direct. Repeat every 3–4 weeks
- [ ] 15 March–15 April: sow marigold and helichrysum. 15 March onwards: sow candytuft direct
- [ ] 15–24 March: harden off sweet peas. Mid-March: buy planters S1 and S2
- [ ] 25 March–5 April: plant out sweet peas, one plant per cane every 15 cm: 24 Spring Sunshine into S1 and 24 of the others into S2 (24 canes in each). The other 26 go to your garden
- [ ] Strawberries arrive: plant at once (by 31 March), net the bed
- [ ] Apply slug nematodes once soil is above 5 °C
- [ ] 25 March–10 April: pot up dahlia tubers into 3 L pots
- [ ] Put netting up for supported flowers (larkspur, stocks, cornflower)
- [ ] Fleece alliums and chives again (leaf miner, Mar–Apr). Plant mint and applemint in sunk pots

## April

- [ ] 1–10 April: plant out ranunculus batch 2 (harden off from 25 March). Sow Red Kalibo. Plant out onion clumps (5–15 April)
- [ ] 1–20 April: pot up peppers. 10–25 April: pot up tomatoes. 1–15 April: pot up eucalyptus
- [ ] 12–20 April: plant out stocks batch 2. Mid-April: average last frost
- [ ] 15 April onwards: plant out achillea, rosemary, chives and thyme (buy plants). Plant out scabiosa (15–25 April) and Didiscus (23–30 April)
- [ ] 25 April: start planting out 'Primo II' (to 15 May)
- [ ] Fleece on tender plants for frosty nights until May
- [ ] Pinch cornflower, clarkia and orlaya
- [ ] 20 April: sow basil indoors (to 10 May)
- [ ] Put insect mesh on hoops over cabbages and carrots

## May

- [ ] Plant out eucalyptus (15–20 May). Plant out lemon bergamot and rudbeckia (10–20 May)
- [ ] 10 May: first sweet pea picks
- [ ] 9–16 May: pull tulip batch 1. Wrap in newspaper, fridge
- [ ] 15–25 May: plant out dahlias. 25 May–10 June: plant out tomatoes. 20–31 May: plant out marigold, statice and helichrysum, once frost-free
- [ ] 20–23 May: pull tulip batch 2
- [ ] Clear the fridge of fruit. Clean buckets and cutters
- [ ] Shade cloth ready if it turns hot
- [ ] 29 May evening: take out tulips, re-cut and stand in cold water
- [ ] 30 May: harvest day. Cut, sear poppies, condition in a cool dark place
- [ ] 31 May: sort and arrange. 1 June: wedding

## June

- [ ] Clear planters T and R once the flowers finish. Plant 8 Red Kalibo in T with marigolds (1–15 June). Sow late carrots and beetroot in R under insect mesh
- [ ] 5–15 June: plant out peppers. 15–25 June: plant out basil
- [ ] Strawberry harvest begins. Net, pick daily
- [ ] Cut back lavender and thyme after flowering
- [ ] 1 June–15 July: sow Lunaria and Iceland poppies for 2028

## July–August

- [ ] Harvest beetroot, carrots, cabbage, onions, tomatoes
- [ ] Cut and hang statice and helichrysum to dry
- [ ] Last carrot sowing by mid-July. Take dahlia and rosemary cuttings
- [ ] Pull the sweet peas in late July. Sow a green manure or winter salads in S1 and S2
- [ ] Order bulbs for next spring

## September–October 2027

- [ ] Lift and dry onions
- [ ] Cut dahlias until the first frost, then mulch 10–15 cm (or lift and store)
- [ ] Pick Red Kalibo. Clear beds, rotate families ([Part 4](#/p4/rotation))
- [ ] Transplant Lunaria (15 September–31 October)
- [ ] Plant next year's bulbs by late October
