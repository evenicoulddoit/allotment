| Orlaya | Half the florets open | None | 3–5 days | 5–7 days |
| Nigella | First flower open | Seed pods last weeks | 2–3 days | 4–5 days |
| Cornflower | Half open | Strip lower leaves | 3–4 days | 5–7 days |
| Poppy 'Bridal Silk' | Bud cracking, colour showing | Sear: dip 2–3 cm of stem end in just-boiled water for 5–10 seconds, then cold water | 1–2 days | 2–4 days |
| Geum | First flowers opening | Plain water | 3–4 days | 5–7 days |
| Thlaspi | Flowers or green seed discs | None | 5–7 days | 7–10 days |
| Eucalyptus | Any time | Strip lower leaves, split woody ends | 1–2 weeks | 2+ weeks |
