Mice, voles, squirrels and pigeons are the four things most likely to cost you flowers before 30 May. The defence has four layers, and each is already built into the layout in [Part 4](#/p4/layout): (1) do not feed or house them, (2) make the plants physically unreachable, (3) put the tastiest plants where they are watched and fenced, (4) monitor and trap.

## Layer 1. Do not feed or house them

- **Store bulbs** in a lidded metal tin or thick plastic box, never a paper bag.
- **Remove hiding places.** Pallets, old carpet, plastic sheeting, stacked timber and long grass beside beds shelter mice. Stand stored items 30 cm off the ground and keep path edges short.
- **Compost carefully.** Use an enclosed bin with a mesh base. Never add cooked food, bread, meat or dairy. Open heaps feed rats.
- **No bird feeders.** Spilled seed brings both rats and mice.
- **Pick up windfalls** and ripe fruit daily, especially strawberries.

## Layer 2. Make the plants unreachable

**Planters T and R (bulbs and corms).** The planters have no base, so build the mesh in from the start: lay 6 mm galvanised mesh on the ground, 30 cm wider than the planter all round, set the planter on it, and fold the mesh up the inside walls 15–20 cm and fix it. Overlap joins by 10 cm and wire them (about 3 m² of mesh per planter). Then, straight after planting, lay a mesh lid flat over the whole bed. 25 mm mesh stops squirrels; finer (6 mm) also stops mice, but shoots must be able to grow through it, so remove a fine lid once shoots are 10 cm tall.

**In open ground** (any bulbs planted outside the planters), after planting lay 25 mm galvanised wire mesh flat over the whole bed and peg it every 20 cm with an extra 15 cm skirt turned down at the edges. Shoots grow through the holes. Leave it on until growth is 10 cm tall, then remove it — or leave it if it does not restrict the leaves. Mesh as fine as 6 mm stops mice from squeezing through, but squirrels are stopped by 25 mm.

**Plant deep.** Tulips 15–20 cm in open ground (your note says 6–10 inches, which matches). In a 25 cm deep planter plant at 10–15 cm: the mesh base and lid do the protecting, and a 15–20 cm hole would leave too little soil below the bulb. Ranunculus corms go only 3 cm deep. Squirrels rarely dig below 10 cm.

**Grit helps drainage, not rodents.** 3 cm of coarse grit around each bulb prevents rot in deep planting but does not deter animals on its own.

**Chilli or capsaicin powders** give a short-term deterrent only. Rain washes them off, so treat them as an extra and not the main defence.

**Sweet pea and poppy seed:** sow into root trainers or modules, put the trays in the cold frame, and cover each tray with mesh as well. Plant out as seedlings.
