## By the end of the window

| By | Job | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 29–31 Oct | Line the cold-frame floor with 6 mm mesh, close every gap | Do this before any seed goes in |
| 29 Oct–10 Nov | Sow all 74 sweet peas into root trainers, mesh over the trays | Soak overnight first. 48 go on the plot, 26 to your garden. 'Spring Sunshine' sow the same week |
| 29 Oct–5 Nov | Sow stocks batch 1 (3 varieties) | |
| 29 Oct–15 Nov | Sow larkspur, clarkia, orlaya, nigella, cornflower, Thlaspi, Bridal Silk poppy | Frame, under mesh. Check the tray ledger: about 14 of 18 trays by 5 Nov |
| 29 Oct–15 Nov | Sow the gambles: Iceland poppies, scabiosa, clary sage (only if Salvia viridis) | These are the first to go if the frame fills |
| 29 Oct | Store tulip batch 2 in a lidded metal tin at 5–9 °C | Check weekly for mould |
| 29 Oct–8 Nov | Buy and build planter T (mesh base, mesh up the sides) | Needed before the tulips |
| 1–8 Nov | Pot up ranunculus batch 1 (about 40 corms) into trays, 3 cm deep | Soak 3–4 hours only |
| By 20 Nov (aim for 8 Nov) | Plant tulip batch 1 (about 48 bulbs) in planter T, 10–15 cm deep. Mesh lid straight away | The rest of T is left for batch 2 in January |
| 29 Oct–15 Nov | Plant your 10 alliums in T between the tulips, and the daffodil ring on the ground round where T and R will stand (leave room for the planter) | 10–15 cm deep |
| 29 Oct–15 Nov | Plant Geum ×5 in bed 4 | Compost, plant at pot depth, mulch |
| From 29 Oct | Fleece or fine mesh over alliums and chives | Leaf miner flies in Oct and Nov |
| 29 Oct–15 Nov | Set 6 snap traps in tunnels. Start the weekly 5-minute patrol | |
| Late Nov | First frost: lid on the frame and fleece over the trays | Have the fleece ready before the end of week one |

## October (from the 29th)

- [ ] Buy planter T (180 × 90 cm, 25 cm deep). Lay the 6 mm mesh base, set the planter on it and fold the mesh up the sides ([Part 4](#/p4/planters), [Part 5](#/p5/threats))
- [ ] Plant tulip batch 1 (about 48 bulbs) in planter T, 10–15 cm deep, by 20 November. Lay a mesh lid over at once
- [ ] Plant the daffodil ring round T and R, and the alliums (by 15 November)
