- **Cut early** (before 8 am) or in the evening. Flowers are full of water then.
- **Prepare clean buckets.** Wash with dilute bleach (or Milton) and fill with 15–20 cm of cool, clean water and flower food.
- **Cut at an angle** with clean, sharp snips. Strip leaves that would sit below the waterline.
- **Plunge straight into water.** Do not leave stems on the grass for more than seconds.
- **Condition for 12–24 hours** in a cool, dark, draught-free place (garage, 8–12 °C), away from fruit, which gives off ethylene.
- **Mon 31 May (bank holiday):** re-cut, sort by variety and stem length, arrange or box.
- **Tue 1 June:** the wedding.

## Keep stems fresh longer

- Re-cut 2 cm every 2 days and change the water.
- Keep away from fruit bowls and radiators.
- Use flower food, or a pinch of sugar and 2 drops of bleach per litre.
- A cool room (below 15 °C) doubles most vase lives.
