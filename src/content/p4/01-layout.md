**Assumptions.** There is no gate (the plot has no physical boundaries), so the south end, where the main path starts, is treated as the entrance. Tall things at the north; tulip and ranunculus planters nearest the entrance; the brassica family in one block; carrots beside onions; compost in the far corner from the bulbs. Paths: main path 1 m, side paths 0.6 m, wood chip over cardboard; a 0.3 m mown edge strip.

Of the 125 m², the beds below total about 60 m² and the planters 6.5 m². The old budget of 95 m² of beds was generous for the plant numbers. **Perimeter** is the length of edge round each bed — use it to price edging boards, mesh skirts or netting. Bed dimensions are taken from the drawing: confirm them on the ground before you buy.
