This part holds your calendar, the cold-frame ledger and the stem forecast. Read them in this order:

1. The **by-job calendar** — what happens to each plant, and when.
2. The **cold-frame ledger** — what is in the frame, and when it is full.
3. The **stem forecast** — roughly how many stems you can expect on 30 May.
4. The **countdown** — the same plan counted backwards from harvest day.

The plot layout is in [Part 4](#/p4/layout). Every chart on these pages is drawn from the same data as the tables beside it, so they cannot disagree.
