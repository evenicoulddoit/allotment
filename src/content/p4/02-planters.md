**Why this order:** tulips are the most dug-up crop and cannot wait. Ranunculus and sweet peas live in the cold frame until March (74 sweet peas are sown; 48 go on the plot, 26 to your garden).

**Money-tight route:** buy T now and R in February. Plant the 48 plot sweet peas in a compost-enriched trench at the north end, in two rows with one plant per cane, with 6 mm mesh collars until May.

## Extenders

Skip them. In a planter the mesh lid protects tulips, so plant them at 10 to 15 cm (open ground: 15 to 20 cm). Ranunculus 3 cm. Sweet peas are fine in 25 cm. Only a planter shallower than about 20 cm justifies one.

## Building each planter

1. Level the spot. Lay 6 mm galvanised mesh on the ground, 30 cm wider than the planter all round, and set the planter on it.
2. Fold the mesh up the inside walls 15 to 20 cm and fix it. Overlap joins by 10 cm and wire them. About 3 m² of mesh per planter.
3. Fill with about half topsoil from the plot, a third compost and the rest grit (about 0.5 m³ per planter at 25 cm). A 25 cm planter is roughly half a cubic metre.
4. For T and R, lay a mesh lid flat over the bed straight after planting and peg it every 20 cm; 25 mm stops squirrels, finer also stops mice but shoots must still push through. Remove it at about 10 cm of growth.
