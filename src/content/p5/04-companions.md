Companion effects are **modest**, and netting does the heavy lifting. These pairings are still worth using because they cost nothing.

| Pairing | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Nigella, orlaya and marigold beside brassicas, stocks and sweet peas | Feed hoverflies and lacewings, whose larvae eat aphids |
| Rosemary, thyme and sage beside cabbages | Scent may confuse egg-laying butterflies (partial effect) |
| French marigolds with tomatoes | May deter whitefly. Also attracts hoverflies |
| Basil with tomatoes and peppers | Scent confuses some insects, and the two ripen in the same conditions |
| Daffodils and alliums around bulb beds | Rodent-proof border (strong effect) |
| Mint in sunken pots near cabbages | May deter cabbage white butterflies (partial effect). Never plant mint loose |
